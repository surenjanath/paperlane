Writing tips

The model is the one chosen in My workspace › AI & models. With no model set, the built-in planner drafts the workflow instantly and offline. A local model can take a minute.

  • Start with a name and a colon: “Leave request: …”
  • One sentence per step: “Manager approves.”, “HR records the leave balance.”
  • List what the first person fills in: “…submits a request with item, amount and reason.”
  • Conditions: “If amount is over 5,000, finance approves.”
  • Reviews can send work back; approvals can reject. Add “Email the requester when done.” for a confirmation.
