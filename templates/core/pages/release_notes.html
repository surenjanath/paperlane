2.0Nov 6, 2026Latest

Paperlane: a new name and a new look

  • Improved
    The product is now Paperlane, with a new logo, a dark navigation rail, warm paper tones and the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface.
  • Improved
    Settings and commands use the new name: PAPERLANE_* environment variables, paperlane_tick and paperlane_mcp.
  • Improved
    Demo data uses dates a few weeks ahead of the day you seed it, so nothing starts out overdue.
  • New
    Bank loan example (seed_bank_demo): a complete personal-loan process connected to a sample core-banking database, from the public application to disbursement.
  • New
    Database connectors: look things up in a read-only SQLite file with a single SELECT; typed values are always query parameters.
  • Fixed
    Formulas with powers (**), such as loan payments, now give the same result while typing as after saving; huge exponents are refused.
  • New
    seed_stories: five demo workflows with named teammates and documents at every stage, to see work move from person to person.
  • Improved
    Steps sent to a teammate's email show their name. People outside the workspace still show as an address.
  • Improved
    Templates on the home page are shown as a grid of cards with a preview of each form.
  • Fixed
    In Approvals, a "Not approved" option was shown as a positive button. Negative choices now always show as negative.
  • Fixed
    Privacy, terms and help pages showed two headers when signed out.

Key dates, a client portal and an AI agent

  • New
    Word templates: generate filled .docx files (and PDFs with LibreOffice) with placeholders and {if} blocks.
  • New
    Verify the recipient with a code by email, text message or an access code, and one-tap decisions straight from the invitation email.
  • New
    Save to cloud drive: Google Drive, OneDrive and SharePoint, in folders named from fields.
  • Improved
    Insights: where documents stop, week-by-week deadline success and slowest step, and CSV export.
  • New
    ✦ Ask your documents: find documents by meaning (with an embedding model) or words, with an AI answer that cites them.
  • New
    Watchdog flags documents with unusual amounts or repeated people, and ✦ This week summarises the team's week on Team workload and in the team report.
  • Improved
    Bots can run in the background with automatic retries; uploads can live on S3-compatible storage; optional Sentry error reporting; tests run on PostgreSQL in CI.
  • Fixed
    On PostgreSQL, creating workflows failed (token columns too short), long imported field ids broke field history, and API calls could drop the database connection.
  • New
    Key dates and reminders: track renewals, expiries and reviews from finished documents, with emails before each date and a Key dates page.
  • New
    Client portal: recipients confirm their email and see everything waiting for them, plus PDFs of what they finished.
  • New
    AI agent bot: describe what should happen in plain English; it sets only the fields you list and adds tags, comments or alerts only if you allow them.
  • New
    ✦ Write with AI for step invitation emails, in a friendly, formal or short tone.
  • New
    MCP tools for AI assistants: check_workflow, explain_workflow, workflow_activity and list_key_dates.
1.7Oct 25, 2026

Checks, undo everywhere, and steadier AI

  • New
    Checks in the builder find jumps, branches and bots that point at deleted steps, rules and email tokens that read fields that no longer exist, branches that can never run and steps nobody is assigned to. Problem steps are marked on the diagram, and each item can be opened, shown on the canvas or handed to the AI to fix.
  • New
    Undo and redo for every builder change (↶ ↷, ⌘Z), including AI changes. The last 30 changes are kept.
  • New
    Find a step (/), fit to screen (F), ⌘/Ctrl + scroll zoom, and blueprint export from the builder menu.
  • New
    Review before publishing: what changes since the live version, warnings, documents still running on the old version, and a note for the audit trail.
  • New
    Live activity on the diagram: documents waiting and overdue at each step, and how long the step usually takes.
  • New
    ✅ Approval in the + menu adds an approver, an Approve / Reject field and an end-on-reject branch in one click. Branches can be collapsed.
  • New
    AI assistant: Refine proposal for follow-ups, and Explain this workflow in plain English to copy and share.
  • New
    Daily AI limit per workspace in AI & models.
  • New
    Step templates: save a step with its fields and bots and add it to any workflow. Replace a recipient swaps an email across a whole workflow. Fix all with AI in Checks.
  • New
    Path tab on every document: the workflow diagram with what was done (by whom, how long), where it waits now and what is still ahead.
  • New
    Discard changes from the publish review, and restoring an old version can now be undone.
  • Improved
    Pages keep a readable width on large screens (dashboards and boards still use the full width); quieter tag rows on document cards; tidier checkbox labels.
  • Improved
    Ask AI and help-centre answers appear as they are written instead of after the whole reply, with every provider; if streaming isn't available the full answer still arrives.
  • Fixed
    Form values sent to AI are fenced off as data, so text in a field can't give the model new instructions. Version comparisons no longer mark every later step as moved when one step is inserted.
  • Improved
    AI assistant: the model in use is shown, a progress card with Cancel replaces the bare timer, suggested requests name your own steps and fields, the review starts from the problems Checks found, and a suggestion can be proposed in one click.
  • Improved
    AI & models shows times in seconds, feature names instead of codes, and advice from the last week's calls (timeouts without a backup, busy providers, broken replies, slow models).
  • Fixed
    AI replies with misplaced or missing brackets are rebuilt instead of failing; busy providers (429, 5xx) are retried; NVIDIA and custom endpoints ask for JSON replies. The fill, review and translate bots and fill-from-text now repair a bad reply instead of failing.
  • Fixed
    Restoring a version, undoing an AI change or duplicating a workflow no longer drops a step's contact recipient or a document's uploaded file, and an imported file can't link a group from another workspace.

Simulate before you publish, start from email, AI form builder

  • New
    Workflow simulator: test documents run through every branch, jump and bot route, then are undone. It reports unreachable steps, groups with no members, jumps that do nothing and routes that loop.
  • New
    Start from an email: forward a message to a workflow's address; the AI fills the first step, attachments are saved and the sender gets a link to check and submit.
  • New
    AI builder in the document and start-form editors: build from a description or an existing PDF, Word file or photo, edit by instruction, and review with one-click suggestions.
  • New
    Sensitive fields that are never sent to AI and are masked for people not involved, in exports too.
  • New
    Field history on every document, saved views on Documents, a field library of reusable sections, and quick approvals on your phone.
  • New
    Model test bench and an optional fast model for quick AI tasks.
  • Improved
    New landing and sign-in pages. On phones the icon rail becomes a bottom bar and the menu opens as a panel.
  • Improved
    AI answers in broken JSON are repaired automatically; AI fills tables into the right columns.
  • Fixed
    The menu button was hidden on small screens. Bots skipped by their conditions no longer reload the document, which speeds up large workflows.

Assistant everywhere, “What went wrong?” and support tickets

  • New
    “What went wrong?” — the assistant diagnoses a document (failed bots and how many documents they affect, stuck or expired steps, missing recipients, unsent invitations, overdue work, failed webhooks) and explains the fix. A banner appears on documents with problems, and failed rows in Bots logs link straight to it.
  • New
    Support tickets — High and Critical problems file a ticket automatically, one per problem; it's updated as more documents are affected and reopened if the problem returns. Tickets page with notes and status, plus a platform-wide queue for super admins.
  • New
    Help center — 35+ articles across 10 topics, instant search and an AI chat that answers from the articles and links its sources. The assistant is available on every page (✦ in the rail) and knows which document you're looking at.
  • New
    Demo accounts on the login page in development — one click to sign in as any role.
  • Improved
    Team, groups and contacts — summary cards, role pills you can change in place, open work and last-active per person, side panels with details, groups that show where they're used and warn when a used group has no members, contacts with document history.
  • Improved
    Audit trail — categories, people and workflow filters, sensitive-action filter, daily activity chart, grouping of related events and a richer CSV export.
  • Improved
    Landing and sign-in pages redesigned; Release notes, Terms and Privacy rewritten.
