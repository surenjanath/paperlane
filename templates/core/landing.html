Questions

More in the help center.

Do the people filling in forms need an account?

No. They open a secure link from their email, or your public form, and fill or sign right away. Only your team needs accounts.

Does the AI send my documents to the cloud?

Only if you choose a cloud model. With a local Ollama model every AI feature runs on your own machines. Signatures, files and fields marked sensitive are never sent to any model.

Can I bring workflows from another system?

Yes. Import a configuration export and the steps, documents, groups, bots and conditions are recreated for you to review before publishing.

What happens when something fails?

The document shows a banner. Ask the assistant what went wrong and it explains the cause and the fix. High and critical problems open a support ticket automatically.

Can it connect to our other systems?

Use custom connectors for any HTTP and JSON API, signed webhooks, the REST API, or the MCP endpoint with a personal token.

Are the signatures legally binding?

Paperlane records consent to sign electronically, who signed, when and from where, and seals a certificate onto the final PDF. Check the rules for your jurisdiction and document type.
